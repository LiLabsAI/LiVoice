LiVoice

Delete your account

Account deletion is available inside the LiVoice Android app so the request can be tied to your signed-in device and a fresh email verification.

How to delete

  1. Open LiVoice.
  2. Open Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and complete the new email code.

What is removed

LiVoice deletes active sessions, all synced history, dictionary, and analytics records, and identifiable usage records. Local data assigned to that account is removed from the device. Audio and raw ASR text are never retained.

If you lost your device

Install LiVoice on another Android device, sign in with the same verified email, then use Account deletion.
