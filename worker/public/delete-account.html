LiVoice

Delete your account

Account deletion is available inside the LiVoice app on Android, macOS, and iOS so the request is tied to your signed-in device and a fresh email verification.

How to delete

  1. Open LiVoice on your Android phone, Mac, or iPhone.
  2. Open Settings, then Account.
  3. Select Delete account and complete the fresh six-digit email verification code.

What is removed

LiVoice immediately deletes active sessions, pending sign-in challenges, all synced history, dictionary, and analytics records, and identifiable usage records. Local data assigned to that account is removed from the device. Audio and raw speech recognition output are never retained.

If you lost your device

Install LiVoice on another Android, macOS, or iOS device, sign in with the same verified email address, and then use Delete account in Settings. If you need assistance, contact support@aliahad.com.
